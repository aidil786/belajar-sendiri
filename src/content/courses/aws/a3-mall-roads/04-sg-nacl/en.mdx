<Card id="see-nacl" type="see">
A **<Term id="network-acl">network ACL</Term>** is the gate of the whole row. It remembers nothing. A shopper got in on 443. Their reply leaves to port 50000. Who needs a rule for it?

<Interactive name="Choice" options={[{ label: 'The row gate (network ACL), an Out rule', correct: true, why: 'It forgets who came in. It checks every packet.' }]} />
</Card>

<Card id="deeper" type="deeper">
The default network ACL allows everything, so most people only tune security groups. A new security group lets nothing in and everything out. Instead of SSH, try Session Manager: it needs no open inbound port at all.
</Card>
