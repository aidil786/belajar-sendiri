<Card id="deeper" type="deeper">
All three need CI first. Continuous deployment needs the most trust in tests. In GitHub, a required reviewer is the “person says go” step. Free plans allow it in public repos.

Real rep (optional): in a public repo, open Settings, then Environments. Add `production` with you as reviewer. Then add `environment: production` to a deploy job.
</Card>
