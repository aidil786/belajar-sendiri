<Card id="try-swipe" type="try">
Your test is flaky. Good fix, or needs work? Swipe each one.

<Interactive name="SwipeJudge" prose cards={[{ text: 'Press re-run until it turns green', good: false, why: 'The flake stays. </Card>

<Card id="deeper" type="deeper">
Many teams aim for CI feedback in about ten minutes. To go faster: caches, jobs side by side, and path filters (World C2). GitHub can re-run only the failed jobs. Handy, but still fix the flake.

Real rep (optional): open your repo's last ten runs. Did one commit go red, then green, with no change? You found a flaky test.
</Card>
