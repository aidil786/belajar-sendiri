<Card id="deeper" type="deeper">
Web addresses and keys differ per place. They come in at deploy time. In GitHub, `environment:` on a job picks that place's secrets and rules. Container images make this easy: one image, many places (World C2).

Real rep (optional): find where your repo builds. Does a deploy job build again? Pass the build on with `actions/upload-artifact` and `actions/download-artifact` instead.
</Card>
