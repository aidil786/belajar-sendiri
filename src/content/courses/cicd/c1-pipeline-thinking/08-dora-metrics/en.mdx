<Card id="deeper" type="deeper">
DORA began with four metrics in 2014. In 2023, the old “time to recover” became failed deployment recovery time. In 2024, DORA added deployment rework rate. Measure one app at a time. Watch the trend, not one bad day.

Real rep (optional): count your repo's deploys last month. That is your deployment frequency.
</Card>
