A **<Term id="cve">CVE</Term>** is a public ID for a known hole, like `CVE-2099-0001`. Scanners give each one a severity. Put them in order, mildest first.

<Interactive name="OrderList" items={['LOW', 'MEDIUM', 'HIGH', 'CRITICAL']} label="Mildest at the top" success="CRITICAL is the one that stops the line." />
</Card>

<Card id="deeper" type="deeper">
`ignore-unfixed: true` skips holes that have no fix yet. Docker Scout does the same job with `docker/scout-action`. Old images get new CVEs too. Scan them again on a schedule.

Real rep (optional), with Trivy installed from its official docs:
