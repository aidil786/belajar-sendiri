<Card id="deeper" type="deeper">
Where the story breaks. A real belt moves one pack at a time. A pipeline runs many jobs at once. Each worker forgets everything after its job. And a tag is not glued on. It can move to another pack. Only the digest (`sha256:…`) never changes.
</Card>
