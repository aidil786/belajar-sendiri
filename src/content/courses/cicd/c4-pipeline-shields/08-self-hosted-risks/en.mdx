<Card id="deeper" type="deeper">
Need your own runners anyway? Keep them for private repos. Make them do one job, then wipe them (just-in-time runners). Use runner groups to limit which repos reach them. Keep secrets and cloud access on the machine to a minimum. Even then, other jobs there may see secrets passed on a command line.
</Card>
