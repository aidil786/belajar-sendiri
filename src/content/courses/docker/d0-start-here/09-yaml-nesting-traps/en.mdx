<Card id="try-indent" type="try">

<Card id="try-zero" type="try">
Look at the box: the reader sees a number, 123456789. The first 0 is gone! Phone numbers are text. Quotes keep the 0.

<Card id="deeper" type="deeper">
Tools use different YAML readers. Older readers (YAML 1.1) turn yes, no, on and off into true or false. Newer ones keep them as words. Quotes are safe everywhere.

Real rep (optional): in your code editor, turn on “show whitespace”. Tabs and spaces look different then. Open a YAML file and check it.
</Card>
