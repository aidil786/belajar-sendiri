Proof! The page left lunch1 only. lunch2 still has it. `docker exec` runs a command inside a box. Lesson 6 has more.

<Card id="win" type="win">
You can now tell a meal pack (image) from a lunchbox (container).
</Card>

<Card id="deeper" type="deeper">
A container is its image plus a thin writable layer on top. Every change goes into that layer. Remove the box, and its layer goes too. The image underneath stays untouched.
