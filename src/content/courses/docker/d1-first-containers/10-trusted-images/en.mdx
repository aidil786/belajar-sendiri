<Card id="deeper" type="deeper">
Look for the <Term id="official-image">Docker Official Image</Term> and <Term id="verified-publisher">Verified Publisher</Term> badges. Docker also offers Docker Hardened Images, extra-small safe bases (World D2). Docker Hub limits pulls when you are not logged in. World D5 explains.
