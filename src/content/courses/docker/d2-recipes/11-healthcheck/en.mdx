<Card id="deeper" type="deeper">
Defaults: every 30s, 30s timeout, 3 retries. `--start-period` gives a slow app time to wake up. Compose can wait for `service_healthy` (World D4). Kubernetes ignores HEALTHCHECK and uses its own probes (Part 3). Check `/` or a small `/health` page, never anything slow.
