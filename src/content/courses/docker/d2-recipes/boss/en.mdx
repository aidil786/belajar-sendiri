<Card id="deeper" type="deeper">
Why the `chown` line? nginx writes its cache to `/var/cache/nginx` and its process id to `/run/nginx.pid`. As root, it may. As `nginx`, only if it owns them. Some platforms block low ports for normal users. Many teams use 8080.
