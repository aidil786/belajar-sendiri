<Card id="deeper" type="deeper">
Run-time env vars still show in `docker inspect`. Compose secrets arrive as files in `/run/secrets`.

Where the story breaks: a locker isn't truly outside. Volumes sit on the same disk, or in Docker Desktop's VM. Lose the disk, lose the locker. Phone lines are only software. And an env var is no locked box.
