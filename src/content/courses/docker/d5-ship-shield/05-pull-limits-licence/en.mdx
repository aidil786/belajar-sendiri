<Card id="deeper" type="deeper">
Free tiers change, so check docs.docker.com before you plan. A pull counts when you fetch an image's manifest. A HEAD request, which only peeks, does not count. In CI, log in, or keep copies in your own registry. Podman, and Docker Engine on Linux, are free choices too.
