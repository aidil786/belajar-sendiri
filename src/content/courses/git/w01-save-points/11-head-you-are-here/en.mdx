<Card id="see-move" type="see">

<Card id="deeper" type="deeper">
Git keeps HEAD in a tiny file, `.git/HEAD`. Most of the time it says `ref: refs/heads/main`, which means "HEAD points at main". In World 2, you will move HEAD onto an old commit. That is called a detached HEAD.
</Card>
