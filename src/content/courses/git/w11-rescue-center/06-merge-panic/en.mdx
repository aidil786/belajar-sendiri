<Card id="deeper" type="deeper">
The merge worked, but you regret it? Not pushed: `git reset --hard ORIG_HEAD` jumps back to just before it. Careful, that wipes uncommitted changes. Already pushed: `git revert -m 1 HEAD` makes an undo commit and keeps main's side. Fixing conflicts step by step is in World 3.
</Card>
