<Card id="check-review" type="check">
A reviewer asks for changes. Judge each reply.

<Interactive name="SwipeJudge" prose cards={[{ text: '"Thanks! Fixed in my latest commit."', good: true, why: 'Short, kind, and it shows the fix.' }, { text: '"It works on my machine, so no."', good: false, why: 'Try their case first. <Card id="deeper" type="deeper">
Every October, Hacktoberfest celebrates open source. Many projects tag friendly issues for it. Rules change each year, so read hacktoberfest.com first. Was your PR closed without a merge? That happens, often for reasons outside your control. You still walked the whole path. Pick another issue.
