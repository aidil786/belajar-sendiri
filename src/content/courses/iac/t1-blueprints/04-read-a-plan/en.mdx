<Card id="deeper" type="deeper">
`# forces replacement` marks the setting that causes a `-/+`. A `+/-` means the new one is made first, then the old one goes. `tofu plan -out tfplan` saves a plan, and `tofu apply tfplan` builds exactly that, nothing else. The summary line always ends the plan: `Plan: 1 to add, 0 to change, 1 to destroy.`
</Card>
