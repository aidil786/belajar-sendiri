<Card id="deeper" type="deeper">
The state is JSON. Its `serial` number goes up after each change, and `terraform.tfstate.backup` keeps the one before. `tofu show` prints the whole state in a readable way. On a team, the state lives in a shared backend with a lock. Then two people can't build at once. World T3 covers that.
</Card>
