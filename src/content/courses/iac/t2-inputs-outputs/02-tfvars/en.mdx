<Card id="deeper" type="deeper">
Any file ending in `.auto.tfvars` loads by itself too, in name order. `export TF_VAR_tables=6` gives `var.tables` a value from your shell. In a pipeline, `-input=false` stops OpenTofu from asking. A missing value then fails at once, instead of waiting forever.
</Card>
