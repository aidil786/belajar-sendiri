<Card id="deeper" type="deeper">
`random_pet.helper[*].id` lists every copy's id; that `[*]` is a splat. `count = var.open ? 1 : 0` turns a resource on or off. For a list that changes, `for_each` names each copy by its item instead of a number. That is the next lesson.
</Card>
