<Card id="deeper" type="deeper">
Other backends: `gcs`, `azurerm`, `pg`, `kubernetes`, `http` and more. Terraform also offers HCP Terraform (the `cloud` block). OpenTofu can also encrypt the state itself. After a move, OpenTofu empties the old local file and keeps a `.backup` copy.

Real rep (optional): move a register on your own machine. No cloud, no cost.
