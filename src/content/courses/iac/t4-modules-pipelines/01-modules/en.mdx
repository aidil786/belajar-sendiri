A **<Term id="module">module</Term>** is a stall design you can reuse: a folder of `.tf` files. Variables go in, outputs come out.
</Card>

<Card id="deeper" type="deeper">
Modules can call other modules. Keep them small and boring: one clear job each. A module with one resource and ten inputs is usually just extra typing. `tofu init` sets up modules too. `tofu get` fetches modules only.
