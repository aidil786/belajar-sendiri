<Card id="deeper" type="deeper">
In CI the plan needs the shared backend (T3·1). Without it, the register looks empty. Posting the plan as a comment needs `pull-requests: write` and a small script. For apply after merge, save the plan with `-out` and apply exactly that file. Atlantis or HCP Terraform can run this loop for you. Mission 8 puts this workflow on your repo.
</Card>
