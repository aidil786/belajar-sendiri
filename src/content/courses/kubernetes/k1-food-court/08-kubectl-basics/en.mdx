Your boss says the shop is acting weird. You have one tool: kubectl. What do you do first?

<Interactive name="Choice" options={[{ label: 'Look: kubectl get pods', correct: true, why: 'Always look first. The trays live in the cluster.' }]} />

<Card id="see-get" type="see">
<Interactive name="KubeReplay" scenario="k1-first-pod" commands={['kubectl apply -f tray.yaml', 'kubectl get pods']} label="kubectl apply -f tray.yaml creates pod/tray. kubectl get pods shows it Pending, 0/1: still getting ready." />

**<Term id="kubectl">kubectl</Term>** is your walkie-talkie to the office. `kubectl apply -f tray.yaml` puts up the order in that file. `kubectl get pods` lists your trays. Pending means still getting ready.
</Card>

<Card id="try-describe" type="try">
A tray looks odd? Ask for its whole story. Type `kubectl describe pod tray`. Read the Events at the end.

<Interactive name="FoodCourtSim" scenario="k1-kubectl" goal={{ typed: '^(kubectl|k) describe (pods?|po)( |/)tray\\b' }} hints={['Type kubectl describe pod tray and press Enter.']} success="Scheduled, pulled, created, started. The Events tell the story." />

<Hint>describe ends with Events: what happened to this tray, in order.</Hint>
</Card>

<Card id="predict-logs" type="predict">
The kitchen app printed a message. Which command shows it?

<Interactive name="Choice" options={[{ label: '`kubectl logs kitchen`', correct: true, why: 'Yes. logs shows what the app printed.' }, { label: '`kubectl describe pod kitchen`', why: 'describe tells Kubernetes’ story, not the app’s words.' }, { label: '`kubectl get pod kitchen`', why: 'get shows one status line.' }]} />
</Card>

<Card id="see-logs" type="see">
<Interactive name="KubeReplay" scenario="k1-kubectl" commands={['kubectl logs kitchen', 'kubectl exec kitchen -- hostname']} label="kubectl logs kitchen prints: Kedai Kancil is open. kubectl exec kitchen -- hostname prints: kitchen." />

`kubectl logs kitchen` shows what the app printed. `kubectl exec kitchen -- hostname` runs one command inside the tray.
</Card>

<Card id="try-delete" type="try">
Now tidy up. Read the pink bar first: kind-dev, default. Then delete the tray with `kubectl delete pod tray`.

<Card id="deeper" type="deeper">
`kubectl get pods -w` keeps watching. `kubectl logs -f kitchen` follows new lines. `kubectl exec -it kitchen -- sh` opens a shell inside. The practice terminal only explains that one.
