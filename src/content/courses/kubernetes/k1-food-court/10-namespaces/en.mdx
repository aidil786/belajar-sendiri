<Card id="deeper" type="deeper">
`kubectl config set-context --current --namespace=food` makes food your default zone. The pink bar shows it. Careful: `kubectl delete namespace food` deletes everything inside it. Zones keep names apart, not traffic. NetworkPolicies, in World K3, do that.

Real rep (optional): on your kind cluster, `kubectl get namespaces` shows the zones kind made for you.
</Card>
