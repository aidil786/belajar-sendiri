<Card id="try-read" type="try">
Room 3: read the tray’s status 3 ways. First `kubectl get pod tray`. Then `kubectl describe pod tray`. Last, `kubectl get pod tray -o yaml`.

<Interactive name="FoodCourtSim" scenario="k1-tray" goal={{ typed: '^(kubectl|k) describe (pods?|po)( |/)tray\\b', lastTyped: '^(kubectl|k) get (pods?|po)( |/)tray\\b.*-o ?yaml' }} hints={['Way 1: kubectl get pod tray', 'Way 2: kubectl describe pod tray', 'Way 3: kubectl get pod tray -o yaml']} success="Room 3 cleared! One word, one story, every field." />

<Hint>Finish with the `-o yaml` one.</Hint>
</Card>
