<Card id="try-doctor" type="try">
A Job may not use `restartPolicy: Always`. Read the real error. Pick one of the values it lists.

<Interactive name="YamlDoctor" file="tally.yaml" manifest={'apiVersion: batch/v1\nkind: Job\nmetadata:\n  name: tally\nspec:\n  template:\n    spec:\n      restartPolicy: Always\n      containers:\n        - name: count\n          image: busybox:1.37\n          command: ["sh", "-c", "echo 42 plates"]\n'} solution={'apiVersion: batch/v1\nkind: Job\nmetadata:\n  name: tally\nspec:\n  template:\n    spec:\n      restartPolicy: Never\n      containers:\n        - name: count\n          image: busybox:1.37\n          command: ["sh", "-c", "echo 42 plates"]\n'} keep={['kind: Job', 'restartPolicy:']} hint="Never or OnFailure. Both let the task end." success="Fixed! The count runs once, then rests." />
</Card>

<Card id="see-cron" type="see">
A **<Term id="cronjob">CronJob</Term>** makes a Job on a schedule. Its five fields are minute, hour, day, month and weekday.

```yaml
spec:
  schedule: "30 23 * * *"
```

When does this run?

<Interactive name="Choice" options={[{ label: 'Every day at 23:30', correct: true, why: 'Minute 30, hour 23, any day, any month, any weekday.' }, { label: 'Every 30 minutes', why: 'That would be */30 in the minute field.' }, { label: 'On the 23rd of each month', why: 'The day is the third field. Here it is *, so every day.' }]} />
</Card>

<Card id="try-sim" type="try">

<Card id="deeper" type="deeper">
A failing Job retries, up to `backoffLimit` times (6 by default). Without `timeZone`, a CronJob follows the cluster's clock, often UTC. A CronJob keeps the last 3 good Jobs and removes older ones.

Real rep (optional): on a throwaway cluster, run `kubectl get cronjobs` and `kubectl get jobs`.
</Card>
