<Card id="check-doctor" type="check">
This Service file won't apply. Read kubectl's words, then fix it.

<Interactive name="YamlDoctor" file="service.yaml" manifest={'apiVersion: v1\nkind: Service\nmetadata:\n  name: shop\nspec:\n  selector:\n    app: shop\n  ports:\n    - port: 80\n      targetport: 80\n'} solution={'apiVersion: v1\nkind: Service\nmetadata:\n  name: shop\nspec:\n  selector:\n    app: shop\n  ports:\n    - port: 80\n      targetPort: 80\n'} keep={['port: 80', 'app: shop']} hint="Field names care about capital letters. Look at the last line." success="Applied! port is the order number. targetPort is the tray’s own port." />

Plain words: kubectl does not know a field called `targetport`.
</Card>

<Card id="win" type="win">
You can now give pods one fixed address with a Service.
</Card>

<Card id="deeper" type="deeper">
`port` and `targetPort` can differ: port 80 can lead to a tray's port 8080. On every stall, kube-proxy sends each order to a random ready tray. The practice sim takes turns instead. This plain kind is called ClusterIP: it works inside the cluster only.
