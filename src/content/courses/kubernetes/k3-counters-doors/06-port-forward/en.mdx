<Card id="deeper" type="deeper">
`svc/shop` picks one tray when the hatch opens and sticks to it. If that tray goes away, the hatch breaks: run it again. `--address 0.0.0.0` lets anyone on your Wi-Fi in. Use it only on purpose.
