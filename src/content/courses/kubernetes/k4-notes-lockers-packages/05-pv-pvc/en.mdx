<Card id="deeper" type="deeper">
Careful: kind's `standard` class has `reclaimPolicy: Delete`. Delete the claim, and the PV and its data go too. Use `Retain` for data you must keep. While a pod uses the claim, deleting it waits. The claim shows `Terminating` until that pod is gone.
