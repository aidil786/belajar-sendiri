<Card id="deeper" type="deeper">
Overlays can do more: `images:` swaps a tag, `namePrefix:` renames things, `labels:` adds labels. `configMapGenerator` adds a hash to the note's name, so a change rolls the trays. `kubectl diff -k overlays/prod` shows changes first.
