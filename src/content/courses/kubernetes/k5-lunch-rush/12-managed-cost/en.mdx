<Card id="deeper" type="deeper">
Where the story breaks: in our food court, time jumps ahead and stalls are free. Real HPAs check every 15 seconds and wait 5 minutes to shrink. Real schedulers weigh many more scores. Real stalls are machines that bill by the hour. Prices change often, so read your provider's pricing page.
</Card>
