<Card id="deeper" type="deeper">
There are three modes: `enforce`, `warn` and `audit` (writes to the audit log). Pin a version with `pod-security.kubernetes.io/enforce-version: v1.37`. For a Deployment, the guard only warns. Its ReplicaSet's pods are refused later. You meet that in the boss.
