<Card id="predict" type="predict">
The policy wants your pipeline's seal. Someone pushes a new image under the same tag, `shop:1.4.0`. What happens at gate 3?

<Interactive name="Choice" options={[{ label: 'Refused: the new pack has no seal', correct: true, why: 'A seal belongs to the digest, the exact pack.
