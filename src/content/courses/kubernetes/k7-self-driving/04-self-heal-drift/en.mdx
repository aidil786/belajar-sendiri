<Card id="predict" type="predict">
Now **<Term id="self-heal">self-heal</Term>** is off. Someone scales to 1 by hand. What happens?

<Interactive name="Choice" options={[{ label: 'It stays at 1 and shows OutOfSync until someone presses Sync', correct: true, why: 'Without self-heal, Argo CD reports drift but does not undo it.' }, { label: 'Argo CD puts 3 back at once anyway', why: 'Only with self-heal on. <Card id="deeper" type="deeper">
Self-heal waits 5 seconds by default before it syncs again. Some drift is on purpose: an autoscaler changes replicas. Tell Argo CD to ignore that field with `ignoreDifferences`. Real rep (optional): on a kind cluster, `kubectl scale` a Deployment, then `kubectl apply -f` its file. See the count snap back.
</Card>
