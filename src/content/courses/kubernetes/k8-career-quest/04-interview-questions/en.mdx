<Card id="hook" type="hook">
Interview day at the food court. The tech lead asks: "Container or VM: what is the difference?" Your mind goes blank. Pick your answer.

<Interactive name="Choice" options={[{ label: "A container shares the host's kernel. A VM runs its own whole operating system.", correct: true, why: 'Short and true. A container is a boxed-in process.' }, { label: 'A container is just a smaller VM.', why: 'Close! A VM brings a full OS with its own kernel. A container does not.' }, { label: 'VMs are old, so nobody uses them now.', why: 'Both are used every day. Many clusters run on VMs.' }]} />
</Card>

One more line makes the answer shine. Judge each one.

<Interactive name="SwipeJudge" prose cards={[{ text: '"A VM is a whole house. A container is one room."', good: true, why: 'A picture makes it stick. </Card>

<Card id="try-pipeline" type="try">
Question two: "Design a <Term id="pipeline" course="cicd">pipeline</Term> for your app." Start with the stations. Put them in order.

<Interactive name="OrderList" items={['Lint: check the style', 'Test: run the tests', 'Build: pack the image', 'Scan: look for known holes', 'Push: send the image to the registry', 'Deploy: GitOps updates the cluster']} label="First station at the top" success="That's the belt from Part 2. <Hint>Cheap, fast checks first. Shipping comes last.</Hint>
</Card>

<Card id="predict-fail" type="predict">
Follow-up: "A test fails. What does your pipeline do next?"

<Interactive name="Choice" options={[{ label: 'Stops the line. Nothing ships, and the author is told.', correct: true, why: 'Fast, clear feedback. The belt should stop.' }, { label: 'Runs it again until it passes.', why: 'That hides the real problem. Find out why it failed.' }]} />
</Card>
