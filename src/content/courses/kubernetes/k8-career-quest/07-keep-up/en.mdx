<Card id="try-routine" type="try">
Now build a calm weekly routine. Twenty minutes, then stop. Put the steps in order.

<Interactive name="OrderList" items={["Skim this week's release headlines", 'Check: is anything I use going away?', 'Watch one talk, at most', 'Write one line in your notes', 'Close the tabs. Done for the week!']} label="First step at the top" success="That's a routine you can keep for years." />

<Hint>Skim first, then dig into what affects you. Stop on time.</Hint>
</Card>
