<Card id="try-hosts" type="try">
Teach kedai a new name: `stock.kedai` is 10.0.0.21. Add a line to <Term id="hosts-file">/etc/hosts</Term>.

<Interactive name="LinuxSim" scenario="linux-l3-hosts" goal={{ resolves: { 'stock.kedai': '10.0.0.21' } }} hints={["Type echo '10.0.0.21 stock.kedai' | sudo tee -a /etc/hosts", 'Check with getent hosts stock.kedai']} solution={["echo '10.0.0.21 stock.kedai' | sudo tee -a /etc/hosts"]} success="getent hosts stock.kedai now finds 10.0.0.21." />

<Hint>`tee -a` adds a line. Without `-a`, tee replaces the whole file!</Hint>

<Card id="deeper" type="deeper">
The `300` is the TTL: how long others may remember the answer. That is why a DNS change can take time to reach everyone. `www.kedai.example` is a CNAME: a nickname for kedai.example.
