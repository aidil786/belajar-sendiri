<Card id="deeper" type="deeper">
USE comes from Brendan Gregg, for finding why a machine is slow. RED comes from Tom Wilkie, for services. Grafana’s docs say a good RED dashboard shows how users feel. RED points at symptoms; USE often finds causes.
