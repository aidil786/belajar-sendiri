<Card id="predict" type="predict">

<Interactive name="Choice" options={[{ label: 'So a short blip does not wake anyone', correct: true, why: 'Yes. <Card id="deeper" type="deeper">
A good alert is urgent, needs a human, and needs it now. Everything else goes on a dashboard, a ticket or a chat message. World O3 builds real alert rules and routes them with Alertmanager.
