<Card id="deeper" type="deeper">
Google’s SRE book sets limits: on call at most 25% of the time. Aim for at most 2 incidents per 12-hour shift. Too many pages cause <Term id="alert-fatigue" course="cicd">alert fatigue</Term>. Runbooks and burn-rate alerts come in World O3.
