<Card id="deeper" type="deeper">
Nama job jadi label `job`, dan host:port jadi `instance`. Setiap scrape juga tulis `scrape_duration_seconds`. Latihan sebenar: bilik kawalan kecil di laptop anda. Simpan dua fail ini dalam satu folder.

```yaml
# prometheus.yml
global:
  scrape_interval: 15s

scrape_configs:
  - job_name: prometheus
    static_configs:
      - targets: ["localhost:9090"]
  - job_name: node
    static_configs:
      - targets: ["node-exporter:9100"]
```

```yaml
# compose.yaml
services:
  prometheus:
    image: quay.io/prometheus/prometheus:v3.13.5
    ports:
      - "9090:9090"
    volumes:
      - ./prometheus.yml:/etc/prometheus/prometheus.yml:ro
  node-exporter:
    image: quay.io/prometheus/node-exporter:v1.12.1
    ports:
      - "9100:9100"
```
