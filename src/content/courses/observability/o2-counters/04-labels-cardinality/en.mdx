<Card id="deeper" type="deeper">
The number of series is called **<Term id="cardinality">cardinality</Term>**. Too many series fill memory and slow every query. Keep ids, emails and full URLs in logs or traces. Four matchers: `=`, `!=`, `=~` and `!~`.
