<Card id="predict" type="predict">
Nasi lemak at SS15 goes 0, 30, 60, 90… one step each minute. What does `rate()` say?

<Card id="see-reset" type="see">

</Card>

<Card id="try-increase" type="try">

<Card id="check" type="check">
`rate()` says 0.25 per second. What does `increase()` say over `[10m]`?

<Interactive name="Choice" options={[{ label: '150', correct: true, why: 'Yes. 0.25 × 600 seconds = 150. increase() is rate() times the window.' }, { label: '2.5', why: 'That is 0.25 × 10. The window is 600 seconds, not 10.' }, { label: '0.25', why: 'That is the rate. increase() multiplies by the window.' }]} />
</Card>

<Card id="win" type="win">
You can now turn a counter into a speed with `rate()` and a total with `increase()`.
</Card>

<Card id="deeper" type="deeper">
`irate()` uses only the last two samples: twitchy, good for fast graphs. Both guess the edges of the window. So `increase()` can show 133.33, not a whole number. In Grafana, `$__rate_interval` picks a safe window for you.
