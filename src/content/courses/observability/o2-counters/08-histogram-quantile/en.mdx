<Card id="deeper" type="deeper">
`histogram_quantile` guesses inside a bucket, in a straight line. So pick bucket edges near your SLO, like 0.5 s. The average is `rate(…_sum[5m])` divided by `rate(…_count[5m])`. Real rep: p90 for Prometheus’s own web pages.
