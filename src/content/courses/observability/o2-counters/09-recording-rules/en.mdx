<Card id="deeper" type="deeper">
Where the story breaks: a clerk writes a counter down once. Prometheus copies every number every 15 s, changed or not. It keeps them 15 days by default, then throws them away. And a real counter never resets; an app’s does.

Real rep: save `rules.yml`, then add the lines below to your other two files.

```yaml
# rules.yml
groups:
  - name: kedai
    rules:
      - record: instance:node_cpu_busy:ratio_rate1m
        expr: 1 - avg by (instance) (rate(node_cpu_seconds_total{mode="idle"}[1m]))
```

```yaml
# prometheus.yml: add at the top level, after global
rule_files:
  - rules.yml
```

```yaml
# compose.yaml: add under the prometheus volumes
      - ./rules.yml:/etc/prometheus/rules.yml:ro
```
