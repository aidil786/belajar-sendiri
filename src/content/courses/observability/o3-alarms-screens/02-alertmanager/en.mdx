<Card id="deeper" type="deeper">
Timers on a route: `group_wait` (default 30s) waits for friends before the first message. `group_interval` (default 5m) waits before telling about new bells in a group. `repeat_interval` (default 4h) resends if nothing changed. An `inhibit_rules` entry mutes `target_matchers` while `source_matchers` fire.
