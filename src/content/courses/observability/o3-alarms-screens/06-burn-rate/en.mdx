<Card id="hook" type="hook">
Your SLO: 99.9% of orders work this month. At 20m, 5% of orders fail for 4 minutes. Then it heals. Should someone wake up?

<Interactive name="Choice" options={[{ label: 'No. It used a sliver of the month.', correct: true, why: 'Yes. Four bad minutes spend very little of the error budget.' }, { label: 'Yes. Any error above 0.1% is bad.', why: 'Then the pager rings for every blip. People stop listening.' }]} />
</Card>

<Card id="see-burn" type="see">
Divide the error ratio by the budget (0.001). That is the burn rate.

<Interactive name="PromQLPlayground" scenario="o3-fast-burn" at="80m" initial="job:shop_error_ratio:rate1h" goal={{ resultCount: 1, typed: '/\\s*0\\.001' }} hints={['Add / 0.001 at the end.']} solution="job:shop_error_ratio:rate1h / 0.001" success="About 19 at 80m: the month's budget would be gone in about a day and a half." />

**<Term id="burn-rate">Burn rate</Term>** 1 means the budget lasts exactly the month. Bigger burns faster.
</Card>

<Card id="predict" type="predict">
The burn rate stays at 14.4 for one hour. How much of the month's budget is gone?

<Interactive name="Choice" options={[{ label: 'About 2%', correct: true, why: 'Yes. 14.4 × 1 hour ÷ 720 hours in 30 days = 2%.' }, { label: 'About 14%', why: '14.4 is a speed, not a share. One hour is short.' }, { label: 'All of it', why: 'At 14.4 the whole budget lasts about two days.' }]} />
</Card>

<Card id="try-build" type="try">
Page for the big burn from 61m. Stay quiet for the 4-minute spike. Stop soon after the fix at 91m.

<Interactive name="AlertRuleBuilder" scenario="o3-fast-burn" alert="FastBurn" exprs={[{ label: 'Over the SLO, 5m window', expr: 'job:shop_error_ratio:rate5m > 0.001', why: 'Any small bump rings it, like the spike at 20m.' }, { label: 'Burn 14.4, 1h window only', expr: 'job:shop_error_ratio:rate1h > (14.4 * 0.001)', why: 'The hour remembers the bad part. It rings long after the fix.' }, { label: 'Burn 14.4, 5m window only', expr: 'job:shop_error_ratio:rate5m > (14.4 * 0.001)', why: 'Five minutes is too short. The 20m spike rings it.' }, { label: 'Burn 14.4 in the 1h AND the 5m window', expr: 'job:shop_error_ratio:rate1h > (14.4 * 0.001) and job:shop_error_ratio:rate5m > (14.4 * 0.001)', graph: 'job:shop_error_ratio:rate1h', threshold: 0.0144 }]} fors={['2m']} labels={[{ labels: { severity: 'page' } }]} goal={{ firingAt: ['80m'], quietAt: ['25m', '50m', '100m'], noFlap: true }} solution={{ expr: 3 }} hints={['The long window proves it matters. The short window proves it is still happening.']} success="It pages for the real burn and goes quiet minutes after the fix." />

<Hint>Look at 25m and 100m on the timeline. Which rule is quiet at both?</Hint>
</Card>

Fast burns wake someone. Slow burns wait for the morning.

<Card id="deeper" type="deeper">
These numbers come from Google's SRE workbook, for a 99.9% SLO over 30 days. Save each window's error ratio with a recording rule, like `job:shop_error_ratio:rate1h`. Then the alert stays short and fast.
