A **<Term id="runbook">runbook</Term>** is the fix-it card next to the bell. Put its parts in order.
</Card>

<Card id="try-link" type="try">
Link the runbook from the rule. Add a `runbook_url` line under `annotations`.

<Interactive name="YamlPlayground" file="rules.yml" initial={'groups:\n  - name: kedai\n    rules:\n      - alert: QueueTooLong\n        expr: kedai_queue_length > 50\n        for: 3m\n        labels:\n          severity: ticket\n        annotations:\n          summary: "Queue at {{ $labels.stall }} is {{ $value }}"\n'} expect={[{ path: 'groups.0.rules.0.annotations.runbook_url', type: 'text', hint: 'Under summary, same indent: runbook_url: https://wiki.example.com/runbooks/queue-too-long' }, { path: 'groups.0.rules.0.annotations.summary', type: 'text', hint: 'Keep the summary line.' }]} solution={'groups:\n  - name: kedai\n    rules:\n      - alert: QueueTooLong\n        expr: kedai_queue_length > 50\n        for: 3m\n        labels:\n          severity: ticket\n        annotations:\n          summary: "Queue at {{ $labels.stall }} is {{ $value }}"\n          runbook_url: https://wiki.example.com/runbooks/queue-too-long\n'} hint="Copy the indent of the summary line." success="Every bell now points to its fix-it card." />

<Hint>`runbook_url` lines up with `summary`: ten spaces in.</Hint>
</Card>
