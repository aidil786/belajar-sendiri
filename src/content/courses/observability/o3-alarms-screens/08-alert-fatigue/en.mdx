<Card id="try-flap" type="try">
The queue bounces around 25. The bell flaps on, off, on. Make it ring once, in one stretch.

<Interactive name="AlertRuleBuilder" scenario="o3-flappy" alert="QueueLong" exprs={[{ expr: 'kedai_queue_length > 25' }, { expr: 'avg_over_time(kedai_queue_length[5m]) > 25' }]} fors={['0s', '3m']} keepFiringFor={['0s', '5m']} goal={{ noFlap: true, firingAt: ['13m'], quietAt: ['4m'] }} solution={{ expr: 0, for: 1 }} hints={['A short bounce should not ring. Wait a few minutes first.']} success="One stretch of bell for the real long queue. No flapping." />

<Hint>`for` ignores short bounces. `keep_firing_for` stops the bell from blinking off too soon.</Hint>
</Card>

<Card id="deeper" type="deeper">
Where the story breaks. A real control room has one loud bell. Ours has many quiet ones, with labels, routes, groups and silences. And a real bell never learns. Your alerts should: review them often, and delete freely.
</Card>
