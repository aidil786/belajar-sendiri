<Card id="check-error" type="check">
```text
parse error : queries require at least one regexp or equality matcher that does not have an empty-compatible value. For instance, app=~".*" does not meet this requirement, but app=~".+" will
```

Anda minta `{app=~".*"}`: "semua diari". Loki tolak. Pembetulan mana yang berfungsi?

<Interactive name="Choice" options={[{ label: '{app=~"kedai-.*"}', correct: true, why: 'Betul. <Card id="deeper" type="deeper">
LogQL boleh mengira juga: `sum by (app) (count_over_time({app=~"kedai-.*"} | json | level=~"error|warn" [10m]))` kira baris bermasalah setiap app. Kekalkan label sedikit dan tetap, contohnya `app`. Jangan letak `order_id` dalam label: setiap nilai buat <Term id="log-stream">log stream</Term> baharu.
